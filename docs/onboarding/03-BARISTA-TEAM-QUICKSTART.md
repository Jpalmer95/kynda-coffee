## 1. What Is Kynda Co-Pilot?

You do **not** need to know anything about "AI" or computers to use Kynda’s new system.

Think of **Kynda Co-Pilot** (`/staff/copilot`) as a **smart walkie-talkie and digital bar notebook** that knows every Kynda recipe, cleaning guide, and shift checklist—and rewards you with **XP and real cash bounties ($5–$50)** when your ideas help improve the shop!

### The 3 Golden Rules (Read This First!)
1. **Talk to it like a coworker:** Tap the microphone button 🎙️ (or type in plain English or Spanish). Ask *"How many pumps of lavender go in a 16oz iced latte?"* or say *"How do I hook up a fresh Cold Brew corny keg?"*
2. **You CANNOT accidentally break anything:** You can’t accidentally change menu prices, post to social media, or mess up official company files. Everything important goes to a draft box for Jonathan & Brie to check first.
3. **Know the 3 Standing Kynda Shop Rules:**
   - **Rule #1 (Guests First):** An unfinished opening checklist **NEVER blocks serving guests**. Serve guests with a warm smile first; finish checking off your list around service.
   - **Rule #2 (Equipment Issues):** If an espresso grouphead, grinder, kegerator, sink, or fridge acts up, report it in **`/staff/copilot` (Equipment Issue)** + **Team Chat** so leadership fixes it fast. **NEVER put equipment problems in the Waste Log!**
   - **Rule #3 (Waste Log):** The **Waste Log** (`/staff/waste-log`) is strictly for **food, drink, and inventory items** (spilled milk, expired pastries, dropped cups, remade drinks). Logging waste honestly helps our auto-order system restock before we run out!

## 2. Everything You Can Do (And Where to Tap)

When you sign into **`https://kyndacoffee.com/staff`** on your phone or the bar tablet, here are your tools:

### A. Inside `/staff/copilot` (Your Co-Pilot & Kaizen Hub)

| Button / Pill | When to Use It | What Happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🔊 Play 60s Shift Brief** *(top right)* | Right when you clock in for your shift. | Speaks a 60-second audio summary of today's espresso dial-in, active specials to recommend, and any equipment notes. |
| **✨ Smart Walkie-Talkie (Auto-Route)** | Anytime you have a question during a shift. | Answers instantly from our 91 official Kynda recipes, equipment How-To guides, and Employee Handbook. |
| **💡 Kaizen Idea ($5–$50 Bounty)** | Whenever you spot a way to make a station faster, prevent a rush stockout, save waste, or make guests happier! | Scores your idea (`0–100`), gives you **+25 Kaizen XP** immediately, and sends it to Jonathan & Brie. When they approve and adopt it into our SOPs, you get **+100 XP + a $5 to $50 bounty**! |
| **☕ Morning Espresso Dial-In** | Every morning when dialing in the bar (or re-dialing mid-day). | Say or type: *"Dial-in: House Espresso 18.5g in, 37g out in 29s"*. It checks if your ratio/time is in spec, offers grind coaching if needed, and awards **+15 XP**. |
| **🔧 Equipment Issue (Leadership)** | Anytime a piece of bar/kitchen equipment is leaking, jamming, or acting weird. | Opens a ticket for Management + Team Chat and awards **+10 XP**. *(Even if you accidentally type an equipment problem under waste, Co-Pilot will catch it and route it here automatically!)* |
| **🔒 Owner Whisper (Jonathan & Brie)** | Anytime you have a private concern, safety note, scheduling worry, or feedback meant **only** for the owners. | Encrypted and locked so **ONLY Jonathan and Brie** can read it (no other baristas or managers can ever see it). You can even check **Submit Anonymously**. |

### B. Your Other Daily Staff Portal Pages (`/staff`)

- **Checklists (`/staff/checklists`):** Check off Opening, Mid-Shift, and Closing duties as you complete them.
- **Recipes (`/staff/recipes`):** Browse and search all 91 drink, food, and seasonal recipes with exact measurements.
- **How-To Guides (`/staff/how-to`):** Step-by-step visual guides for cleaning the espresso machine, grinders, cold brew brewing, and loading corny kegs.
- **Par Counts (`/staff/par-counts`):** Count on-hand HEB and Amazon items on count days so we never run out of milk, syrups, or cups.
- **Waste Log (`/staff/waste-log`):** Log spoiled, spilled, or expired food/drinks in 10 seconds.
- **Team Chat (`/staff/chat`):** Post shift updates, latte art photos, and videos with the whole crew.
- **Training (`/training`):** Complete short modules to level up your **Barista Skill Tree Rank**:
  $$\text{Apprentice} \longrightarrow \text{Barista} \longrightarrow \text{Dial-In Specialist} \longrightarrow \text{Shift Maestro} \longrightarrow \text{Kaizen Architect}$$
